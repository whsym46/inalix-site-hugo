The "Development & Implementation of the Flight Information Display System (FIDS) for Ngurah Rai International Airport, Denpasar - Bali" project focused on creating an advanced, real-time display system to manage and present critical flight information across the airport. The FIDS integrated seamlessly with existing airport systems, providing passengers and staff with accurate, up-to-the-minute flight data, including arrivals, departures, gate changes, and delays. This user-friendly system was deployed across multiple terminals and key areas, enhancing the overall passenger experience, streamlining airport operations, and improving the efficiency of information dissemination throughout the airport.

#### Case Details

* Client Name: Angkasa Pura I
* Airport Location: I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport - Denpasar Bali
* Started: November 2004
* Category: Flight Information Display System
